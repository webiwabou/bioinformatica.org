Record the standing objective for this project: what the scientist is trying to achieve, what would count as done, which decisions are already fixed, and the stages of the work.

Call this once you and the scientist have agreed what the investigation is — before starting any long-running stage. The objective is written to `.bioinformatica/objective.json` in the project directory and is restated to you at the start of every turn, so it survives context compaction and a fresh session. A campaign outlives a context window; this is what stops you drifting from, or forgetting, what you were doing.

Put every scientific choice the work depends on into `decisions` — thresholds, which fields are searched, what counts as already-known, cutoffs. A long unattended stage cannot stop to ask a question, so anything unrecorded will be silently defaulted.

Call it again to update the objective as the work is refined; it overwrites.
