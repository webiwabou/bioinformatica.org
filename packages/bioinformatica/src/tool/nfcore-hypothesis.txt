Rank a set of candidate hypotheses transparently, for the opt-in brainstorm mode.

Use this ONLY when a scientist has explicitly asked you to brainstorm or generate
hypotheses / next experiments — never as a default response. First reason out the
candidates yourself, then pass them here to be ranked reproducibly.

For each hypothesis provide:
  - statement: the hypothesis, stated as a precise, falsifiable claim
  - grounding: what backs it — "cited" (a source), "computed" (this session's results), or
    "model-inferred" (your own reasoning). Be honest; do not label a guess as cited.
  - proposedTest: how it could be tested, ideally naming a concrete nf-core pipeline/analysis
  - testability, plausibility, novelty: your 1-5 self-assessment of each

The tool returns the hypotheses ranked by a fixed, documented weighting that favours
grounding and testability over novelty (so a shiny but ungrounded, untestable idea cannot
rank first). These are SPECULATIVE candidates, not findings: present them as such, critique
the top ones by reusing the adaptive critique (nfcore_critique) on the proposed analysis,
and let the scientist decide what to test. Never state a hypothesis as an established result.
